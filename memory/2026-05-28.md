### trim_pa_silence.py onset detector v2

Spectral flatness explored but dead end — low-bitrate PA MP3s have near-zero flatness for both noise and voice (librosa returns ~0.000 for everything). Reverted to RMS-based approach with two improvements:

1. **Sustained-voice gate**: candidate onset must persist above sustain_floor for 200ms (70-85% of frames, stricter for noisier files). Room-hum transients don't qualify.
2. **Adaptive threshold + sustain ratio by noise tier**: -40dB+ recordings get higher threshold (14dB) and stricter sustain (85%), -40 to -50 gets 12dB/75%, cleaner gets 10dB/70%.

Tested on 6 FAIL files vs old detector: 5/6 better, 0 worse. 10.mp3 unchanged (genuine hard noise case — 10dB voice-noise gap, hum passes sustain gate). Remaining hard cases left to manual trim.

Rising-energy check tried but regressed on files with soft voice starts — removed.
