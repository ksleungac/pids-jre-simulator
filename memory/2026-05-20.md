## Session: Mouse interactions on route selection screen (Antigravity agent, second session)

Implemented full mouse interactions on the route selection screen (`setup.py`):
- **Hover-to-Highlight**: When the mouse moves inside the route list area, the highlighted route (`selected_idx`) is automatically updated to the route under the cursor.
- **Motion Guard**: Implemented a motion guard checking if `event.pos != self._last_mouse_pos` so that a stationary mouse cursor does not fight/override keyboard navigation (arrow keys).
- **Mouse Wheel Viewport Shift**: Supported both modern `pygame.MOUSEWHEEL` and classic `pygame.MOUSEBUTTONDOWN` (buttons 4 and 5) scrolling. Scrolling shifts the route list viewport (`scroll_offset`). If the mouse is over the route list, it also updates the highlighted route under the cursor; otherwise, it clamps `selected_idx` to the new visible area to prevent viewport snapping/reset.
- **Click-to-Confirm**: Left-clicking (button 1) on any row in the route list immediately highlights the route, loads its full `route.json` data, and confirms/starts the simulation.
