## Folder convention

- `<line>/{pa,sta}/` + `<line>/<diagram>/route.json` = **the shape, on every shipped line.** Diagram = scheduled JR EAST service ID (`1865E`, `1217F`, `1208G`, …). Multi-diagram lines hold one folder per service, and single-diagram lines still get one. `route.json` holds route data only and writes no `audio_root`; absent means the pool → [DATA_FORMAT § audio_root Field](../docs/DATA_FORMAT.md).
- `<line>/<diagram>/{pa,sta,route.json}` (audio beside route.json) = the pre-pool shape, retired 2026-08-08. Only `_joban/tsuchiura` and `_mock/main` still use it.
- **Adding a new line.** Author it into the pool from the start: cut into `audio/<line>/{pa,sta}/` with the filename grammar in [DATA_FORMAT § Pool filename grammar](../docs/DATA_FORMAT.md), put `route.json` at `audio/<line>/<diagram>/`, and write no `audio_root` (absent = pooled). `/pa-make` and `/sta-make` splitter templates already target that shape. A second diagram on the same line then pays only for what is genuinely new, which is the point of the pool.
- **A short measured melody before `sta_cut` is not a defect.** 西国分寺 (`JC17`, 2.9 s of audio before a 6.0 s cut) was flagged during the migration and confirmed correct by the author. Don't re-raise it.
- **Cross-station melody reuse exists but is not dedupable** (~57 MB; 高尾 / 荻窪 / 西八王子 share a tune). No two different stations share an identical file: each STA is a melody plus that station's own closing announcement. Collapsing them would mean splitting the two apart and changing how STA plays. See [#120](https://github.com/ksleungac/pids-jre-simulator/issues/120).
- `_archive/<line>/<diagram>/` = preserved-not-shipped past diagrams. Dredge on glob lookup when adding a new diagram on the same line; old splits are often reusable.
- **A `_`-prefixed route cannot be previewed in atlas mode, by design.** `font_atlas` skips any path segment starting with `_` when it walks its text sources (`font_atlas.py:155`), so an unshipped route's station names are never baked and drawing one raises a declaration error that looks like a regression. `preview_display.py --route _joban/tsuchiura` fails on 土浦 for this reason and always has. The picker, `/build` and the baker all exclude these routes the same way; only a hand-written sweep reaches them.
- `_mock/main/` = curated preview catalog. Details → [`_mock/main/README.md`](_mock/main/README.md).
- Filename-as-store + `_*` prefix rules → [conventions.md § Naming](../.claude/rules/conventions.md).
- **PA filenames are descriptive on every shipped line:** `{station}-{dep|arr}-{direction}`, plus a train-type tier where two diagrams' announcements differ. `{station}` on a `-dep` slug is the previous **stopping** station, which differs from the previous array element on any diagram that skips. `pa_at_station` uses `{station}-stopping[-N]-{direction}`. Numeric slugs survive only under `_joban/` and `_mock/`. Schema reference → [DATA_FORMAT § PA Track Filename Convention](../docs/DATA_FORMAT.md).
- **STA filenames take no direction token.** A melody belongs to a platform, and direction is only a proxy for it. Keihin (`-south`) and saikyo (`-down`) carry one because they were pooled before that rule. They are kept, not copied.

## Per-line notes

Ordered by JR EAST line code. Standard fields omitted (= follows convention). Entries get extended only where line has something IRL-specific or sim-quirky worth recording.

**Every line carries an `Audio state` field, the committed record of what has actually been done to its recordings.** The by-ear gate is the only authority on audio here, and its tool writes verdicts to `audio_src/`, which is gitignored: those verdicts never reach a fresh clone or the other PC. A later session then cannot tell "verified months ago" from "never touched", re-audits a finished corpus with derived thresholds, and reports defects that are not defects (2026-08-08, Saikyo, where the ear then returned 25/25 with every cut unchanged). This field is that missing record.

State it as what was done, with the date, and say **unverified** where nothing is known. An absent verdict is not a pass. Facets worth naming: `sta_cut` placement checked by ear, KAK spliced, silences trimmed, complete melody loops confirmed, PA checked by ear. Update it in the same commit as the work, the same way `validate_data.py` is updated.

The whole-loop rule — keep a whole number of melody loops and trim a trailing partial, at the data's best availability, never as a recut trigger — is recent. It is applied leniently to lines that predate it: an older line unchecked against it is recorded as such, not treated as broken.

### JA — Saikyo (埼京線)

- **Name:** 埼京線 / Saikyō Line
- **Diagrams:** `1349F` (快速 → 川越, through 川越線), `759K` (各駅停車 → 大宮)
- **Sim quirks:** `759K` extends `stops[19..23]` past route-level `dest` (大宮, idx 18) to 川越, as an operational reference with no audio. Same shape as JK `727B`. `1349F` skips 北赤羽, 浮間舟渡, 戸田, 北戸田.
- **Audio quirks: this line's STA is mostly melody with no closing-door announcement.** 18 of 24 files are melody only. `JA25` (北与野) has a full one (2番線、ドアが閉まります), and `JA14`/`JA24` have fragments. `detect_sta_cut.py` reporting `music_end == voice_start` across this line is therefore diagnosing the absent voice, not a misplaced cut: it derives `voice_start` from a level change and finds the next melody block. Cuts land in an inter-loop silence and are correct there, and the whole set passed by ear 25/25 on 2026-08-08. Verify speech with `audio_id.has_speech()`, never a spectral band. A known announcement here reads hf/lf 0.002, identical to melody.
- **大宮 and 川越 carry arrival-type STA.** The `sta` slot on this line is not only departure melodies: 大宮 holds two entries, one of which plays at arrival with the doors open, and 川越 (an arrival terminus) carries the end-of-journey announcement. That makes 川越 an exception to `docs/DATA_FORMAT.md`'s "arrival termini omit `sta`". It is deliberate, an author decision of 2026-08-08.
- **Audio layout:** pooled at `audio/saikyo/{pa,sta}/`. PA slugs carry `-down`, plus a `-kaisoku` / `-kakueki` tier where the two diagrams' announcements differ. The four Kawagoe-line STA files were renamed station-derived (`nisshin-down`, …) because their originals were bare numerics with no station identity; those stations have `sta_code: null`. Grammar → [DATA_FORMAT § Pool filename grammar](../docs/DATA_FORMAT.md).
- **Audio state:** STA: `sta_cut` verified by ear 25/25 on 2026-08-08, every value inherited unchanged. No KAK on this source (measured, and the amplitude path is valid here, with the peak sitting 14–22 dB over the music). PA: checked by ear 2026-08-08, all pass. Silences were not re-trimmed; leads run 0.00–0.40 s and trails to 0.66 s, which is cosmetic.

### JC — Chuo (中央線)

- **Name:** 中央線 / Chūō Line
- **Diagrams:** `1654T` (快速), `916H` (中央特快), `602H` (通勤特快, 立川 → 東京, weekday). 602H is simulated from 立川 because that is where the game's route starts. The service itself originates at 青梅 and runs the 青梅線 in (中央線直通), so those 12 stations are authored as `pre_stops` (青梅 JC62 → 西立川 JC51), the way the two siblings carry 大月 → 相模湖. All three Chūō diagrams are weekday.
- **Audio layout:** pooled at `audio/chuo/{pa,sta}/`. **This is the one line whose PA carries no direction token** (0 of 62 files, against 100 % on every other line). It was pooled 2026-07-26, before that rule existed, and retrofitting means touching every file and both `pa` arrays again. It is 上り, so a reverse (下り) Chūō diagram would collide. Stamp `-up` across all 62 slugs at that point, not before. Two takes of the same announcement are kept apart by a `-{diagram}` tier (`kanda-arr-1654T` / `kanda-arr-916H` / `kanda-arr-602H`), the only line using that form. Every other line's split pairs differ by train type instead. 602H additionally uses a `-tsukin-tokkai` type tier on the three departures that name the train type, so a second 通勤特快 diagram reuses them. **`kokubunji-dep` was renamed to `kokubunji-dep-chuo-tokkai` in the same change**: it served 916H alone, and a bare slug means "one file serves the line" (`docs/DATA_FORMAT.md` § Pool filename grammar), which stopped being true the moment 602H added its own 国分寺 departure. It was also the only 916H departure lacking the tier its five siblings carry.
- **`602H` is cut from two recordings, and the conductor ships.** The exact-diagram recording (`stu9Duc6X8I`) runs on time, so its conductor gives service information: the 8番線 arrival, the 国分寺 快速 connection, the stop list. The author wanted that kept (2026-08-29). It is the source for everything 立川 → 新宿. But that 6:35 run uses **abbreviated announcements and omits every transfer list**, so 四ツ谷 / 御茶ノ水 / 神田 stay as cut from the same recordist's 8:36 `834T` run, which carries them. Both runs say 右側 at all three, so the mix does not contradict itself. 東京 reuses the pool's `tokyo-arr`, which already has the full list. The file → source mapping is in the § sources table below. The (start, end) windows live only in the two splitters, `split_pa_602H.py` and `split_pa_834T.py`, which are under gitignored `audio_src/` and do not travel.
- **`shinjuku-arr-602H` is constructed from five pieces and two recordings.** Neither 602H nor 834T announces 新宿's transfers. The author wanted the information, so 916H's transfer sentences (JA and EN) are grafted into the 602H arrival. 916H is the donor because its sentences sit in their own blocks with ~1 s of silence either side, where 1654T's run into the neighbouring sentence with no gap to cut at. **All three recordings say 「お出口は左側です」 at 新宿, which makes the graft legitimate.** 834T's take says 右側 and could not have been used, so its 新宿 file was discarded rather than kept. The resulting order is 1654T's own (まもなく → お出口 → 次は四ツ谷 → transfers). Built by decode-concat-**re-encode**, never the concat demuxer with `-c copy`: there are four seams across two recordings, and an mp3 bit reservoir does not survive a stream-copy boundary. Seam peaks measure −3.6 to −19.9 dB against the file's −3.1 dB, so there is no burst. It has the same standing as utsunomiya's `higashiwashinomiya_2`: **nothing reasoning about provenance may treat this file as one capture.**
  - **Its first ~25 s is IRL played on passing 中野, not on approach to 新宿** (author, 2026-08-29): the conductor's 「ただいま時刻通り中野駅を通過しております…あと5分ほどで新宿駅に到着いたします。新宿駅は8番線の到着」. The app has no way to fire a PA at a geographic point mid-leg, so it is bundled into the arrival, which is the only slot that exists. This is a concrete case for `TODO.md` § Directions "Auto-drive fires more of what a driver would", where the `d1234`-in-the-filename shape would split this file in two. Don't split it before that mechanism lands: on approach the 8番線 heads-up is still useful, and a fragment with no trigger would never play.
- **F0 separated the two voices on one of these recordings and not the other, so the calibration can come back negative.** On `834T` the conductor read **94 / 145 Hz** against the automated **220–233 Hz** on Japanese windows, and the split at ~185 Hz was clean (utsunomiya's pair sits at 136–145 against 212–225). Its English announcements run lower, to 209.8, so that band is not a floor. On `stu9Duc6X8I` the 青梅線 conductor reads 139 Hz, but the **Chūō-leg conductor reads 217–229 Hz, inside the automated band**. The instrument cannot separate them at all, so the boundary has to come from content and block structure. It cost nothing here because both voices ship, but a future source needing separation cannot assume the method works.

  `834T` sprang two further traps, both a median over a long window hiding a short wrong head. At 新宿's departure the whole window read 253.8 Hz and its first 2.1 s read 82.4 Hz (recorded at `split_pa_834T.py`'s 新宿 entry). On `tachikawa-dep` a 13.8 s window read 212 Hz while its first 9 s was the conductor at 159 Hz. The instrument never caught that one — the author's ear did, and it is why the file carries a hand splice rather than a corrected window.
- **Boundaries on both sources come from block onsets, never from Whisper segment starts.** Both merge the silence ahead of a phrase into the phrase, so `まもなく、新宿` transcribes at 452.76 of `834T` and truly begins at 459.92. Five of nine boundaries were wrong on the first pass for that reason. On `834T` the language-locked pass also returned **nothing at all** for a 12.3 s English announcement: no garbled text, just absence. `audio_id.structure` shows it as a block, and `--speech` on that window alone recovers it.
- **Audio state:** STA: trimmed through `sta-make` Phase B and verified by ear 23/23 on 2026-07-25. 国立's incomplete 2nd loop was spliced, and there is no KAK on this source. `602H` needs no new STA at all: all six of its stops already carry a pooled melody at the siblings' cuts. PA: the original 53 were lead-trimmed and verified by ear 53/53 on 2026-07-25. `602H` was **verified by ear 9/9 on 2026-08-29**, in two passes (4 from `834T`, then 5 rebuilt from the exact-diagram recording). Those nine are cut on block onsets to a 0.08–0.12 s lead, so `trim_pa_silence.py` was deliberately not run over them; this is the line whose loud source made that detector eat 12.96 s of `tachikawa-dep-kaisoku` (`pa-make` § 7.4). `validate_pa.py` passes six of the nine outright and raises three advisory flags: a 0.00 s trail on `ochanomizu-arr-602H` and `shinjuku-dep-tsukin-tokkai`, and a 0.00 s lead on `shinjuku-arr-602H`, whose head is the graft's raw cut. They are left as they are: the ear passed all nine, the pre-existing 53 carry the same flags in greater number, and the skill's own note is that these are suggestions rather than failures. **Two of the nine differ from the splitters' cut, so the mp3s are the truth.** `tachikawa-dep-tsukin-tokkai` lost **3.4 s** of dead air inside the conductor's greeting, and `kokubunji-arr-602H` lost **16.0 s** where the conductor repeated the 「国分寺を出ますと次は新宿に止まります」 warning he had already given ten seconds earlier. Everything else is within 0.08 s of its window, which is frame quantisation rather than an edit, and the four taken from `834T` are byte-identical to their snapshot at `audio_src/chuo/602H/pa_backup_834T/`. `split_pa_602H.py` refuses to overwrite a file whose length no longer matches its window (`--force` overrides), so a re-run cannot silently revert the hand work.

### JK — Keihin-Tohoku (京浜東北線)

- **Name:** 京浜東北線 / Keihin-Tōhoku Line
- **Diagrams:** `1275A`, `727B`
- **Sim quirks:** `727B` extends `stops[41..45]` past route-level `dest` (磯子, idx 40) to 大船, as an operational reference for through-running. The sim terminates at 磯子, the route-level dest, not at `len(stops) - 1`. See [docs/DISPLAY.md § Unified State Machine](../docs/DISPLAY.md).
- **Audio quirks: this line defeats every absolute-dB threshold in the toolchain.** The recordings carry continuous train ambience at **−15 to −37 dB** and are limited flat: PA peaks above 0 dBFS on 59 of 78 files, and STA music sits at ~0 dB with under 1 dB of range. Three separate tools produced confident nonsense before this was noticed:
  - `validate_pa.py` flags 74/78. Its −40 dB gate is *below* the noise floor, so files never trimmed are flagged too.
  - `trim_pa_silence.py`'s `floor+12` gate lands at −3.6 to −25 dB, i.e. above ordinary speech — exactly the Step 7.4 failure mode.
  - `sta-make` Step 7.5's peak-amplitude KAK detector cannot fire, because the KAK here is *quieter* than the music.

  **Use relative or spectral measures on this line, and treat any absolute-dB verdict as unverified.** Per-file loudness also splits hard by diagram (1275A median −10.3 LUFS against 727B's −23.2), but `audio.py` normalizes every file to −15 LUFS at playback, so that gap is inaudible in the app and shows up only in the raw-mp3 verifiers.
- **Audio layout:** pooled at `audio/keihin/{pa,sta}/`. PA slugs carry a direction token (`-south`) and a train-type tier only where the two diagrams keep distinct takes (`-kaisoku` / `-kakueki`). Grammar → [DATA_FORMAT § Pool filename grammar](../docs/DATA_FORMAT.md).
- **Audio state:** PA: trimmed and validated 2026-07-27. STA: **KAK spliced by ear 2026-08-08** across 44 files (43.1 s removed, mean 979 ms), cuts retuned in the same pass, whole-loop rule applied, verified by ear 45/45. Done by hand on the verifier's waveform; the 2026-07-27 auto-splice attempt on this line came out under-tight on 40 of 45 and was reverted. See § Audio quirks for why every threshold in the toolchain misreads this source.
- **新子安 (JK14) plays 鶴見 (JK15)'s melody.** JK14's own recording failed the by-ear gate; the stop references `JK15-south` at 鶴見's cut so it plays identically. The unused take is preserved at `audio/_archive/keihin/sta/JK14-south.mp3`. Author decision 2026-08-08.
